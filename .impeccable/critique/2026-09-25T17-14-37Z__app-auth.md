## Overall Impression

Direction is correct and restrained, but the pages feel unfinished rather than authored — empty teal panel, generic form hierarchy, and signup copy that fights the monitoring product. Biggest opportunity: make auth the cover page of the morning briefing packet, not a soft SaaS split.

## What's Working
1. Cool-paper / ink / thrifty teal discipline; avoids glass, gradient hero, terminal chrome.
2. Shared auth shell keeps Sign In and Create Account one surface; aside copy is monitoring-honest.
3. Sign-in Operate path is short and clear (two fields, one primary action).
