# Auth — Product Showcase Split

Mode: Persuade
Audience: Retail investors signing in or creating an account to open today's briefing
Job: Credentials on a clear form rail beside a TradePilot product-preview showcase that proves watchlist + alerts without becoming a second dashboard
Primary action: Sign in / Create account (shared system for both routes)
Constraints: Preserve logo on form rail only, Better Auth server actions, /sign-in /sign-up, validation, mobile form priority; no glass, dark crypto theme, dashboard chrome, logo/Signal Ticks as decorative motif, or motion for its own sake; showcase uses presentation modules only (not live data)
Approved comp: compositional reference (Nodeka-style hierarchy) translated into Morning Briefing Packet — not a literal visual clone
Mobile reference: compact editorial preview above form; never the full desktop stack

## Direction contract

THESIS: Auth is a credentials rail beside a layered product window into TradePilot — editorial statement + overlapping watchlist/alert/trend modules — not a packet TOC cover, not a dark crypto twin-panel, and not a nested dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Cool paper continuous packet, Castoro for the showcase statement, Source Sans for module UI, thrifty teal links/CTA, clay stamps only on alert marks, hairline strata frames and soft tonal washes for depth; no logo watermark, no Signal Ticks on the showcase, no glass or glow.
STORY: Visitor sees a calm product preview (watchlist rows, price alert stamp, session/trend), fills the form rail, enters the briefing; sign-in and sign-up share one showcase.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Equal split (~50% form / ~50% showcase). Left: logo, title, fields, teal primary — form content max-width restrained within the half. Right: concise Castoro statement above a layered cluster — dominant watchlist hero (~500–560px on large desktop), smaller alert and session/trend modules overlapping its lower edges, thin structural frame/lines, deliberate negative space. Mobile: short editorial line under a rule, then form.
FORM: Layered Product Showcase (composition borrowed from the attached reference hierarchy; materials from Morning Briefing Packet).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: Modules stagger in once; chart line reveals; alert stamp soft-pulses once then settles. prefers-reduced-motion: static. Form is immediate. Sign-in ↔ sign-up keeps the same showcase.
